Refund & Cancellation Policy

Transparent guidelines regarding fee payments, class cancellations, demo sessions, and refund eligibility.

1. Free Demo Session Policy

We offer a complimentary **45-minute Free Demo Class** for new students to experience our teaching methodology before committing to regular monthly tutoring. There is zero financial obligation for demo sessions.

2. Class Rescheduling & Minimum Notice

  • Student/Parent Cancellations: Notice must be provided at least **6 hours in advance** of the scheduled class time to reschedule without session fee forfeiture.
  • Tutor Rescheduling: In the rare event that a class is rescheduled due to unforeseen tutor emergency, a make-up class will be scheduled at the parent's preferred time slot.

3. Tuition Fee Payment Terms

Tuition fees for Home Tuition (Jaipur) and Live Online Batches are billed on a monthly advance or session-based schedule as agreed prior to commencement.

4. Refund Eligibility Criteria

If a parent or student decides to discontinue tutoring services after enrolling:

  • Unused Advance Classes: A pro-rata refund for remaining un-conducted future classes will be processed if cancellation is requested within 7 days of advance fee payment.
  • Completed Classes: Fees for sessions that have already been conducted are strictly non-refundable.
  • Short-term Crash Courses: Specialized short-term board exam crash courses or custom test series modules are non-refundable once activated.

5. Refund Process & Timelines

Approved refunds will be processed via bank transfer or UPI within 5 to 7 business days of written cancellation request approval.
