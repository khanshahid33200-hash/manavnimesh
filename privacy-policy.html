Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to us. Learn how we handle student & parent information securely.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information voluntarily submitted through our website contact form or direct communication, including:

  • Full Name of Parent/Student/School Representative
  • Mobile Phone Number & Email Address
  • Student Grade/Class (6th to 12th) & Academic Board (CBSE/RBSE)
  • Residential Location in Jaipur for Home Tuition scheduling

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used exclusively to schedule tutoring sessions, arrange free demo classes, send progress reports to parents, coordinate online video classes, and communicate regarding educational consultancy inquiries.

3. Data Protection & Non-Sharing Policy

We strictly respect student and parent confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal details to third-party telemarketers or external entities under any circumstances.

4. Parental Consent for Minor Students

For students under the age of 18, tutoring enrollment, scheduling, and progress communications are conducted directly with parents or authorized guardians.

5. Cookies & Local Preference Storage

This website uses minimal browser local storage (`localStorage`) solely to store user visual theme preferences (Light Mode / Dark Mode). No personal tracking cookies are used.
