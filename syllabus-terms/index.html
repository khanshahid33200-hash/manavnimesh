CURRICULUM POLICY

Syllabus Terms & Conditions

Guidelines on syllabus completion timelines, board examination alignment, and supplementary classes.

1. Board Alignment & Curriculum Scope

Tutoring syllabi are meticulously structured in alignment with the official annual curriculum prescribed by the **Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)** and **Board of Secondary Education, Rajasthan (RBSE)** for Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (Classes 6th to 12th).

2. Syllabus Completion Timelines

  • Annual Academic Batches: Targeted to complete full board syllabi 4 to 6 weeks before official school/board examinations to allow dedicated revision time.
  • Crash Courses & Revision Batches: Concentrated modules focusing on past 10-year question paper solving, formula revisions, and high-weightage chapters.

3. Pace Adjustment & Individual Mastery

Teaching speed is dynamically adjusted to ensure student comprehension. If a student requires extra time on complex topics (e.g. Organic Reaction Mechanisms, Calculus, Newtonian Physics), additional time will be allocated prior to moving forward.

4. Supplementary & Doubt-Solving Sessions

Before major unit tests, term exams, or board examinations, extra doubt-solving sessions may be scheduled upon request. Attendance in scheduled revision classes is strongly advised for optimum results.

5. Student Attendance Impact

Frequent unexcused absences or last-minute cancellations by students may compress the remaining syllabus timeline. Parents are encouraged to maintain regular session attendance.
